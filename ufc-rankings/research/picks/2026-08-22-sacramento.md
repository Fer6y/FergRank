## 2. Grading rule (fixed now)

1. **Model vs market**: accuracy + logloss on all odds-matched decisive bouts,
   point-in-time ratings only. Reported by `cardReport.ts`.
2. **Finish projections**: for each scored bout, did it end inside the distance /
   by KO? Compare realised rate to the projected band.
3. **The withdrawn picks are tracked as counterfactuals** — recorded so the
   retraction is testable in both directions. If they win, that is **not**
   evidence the rule works; n=4 against a measured 269-bet ROI of −15.7% is noise.

**Nothing on this card can justify a model change.** n≈10 bouts. Any future
scoring-mechanism proposal goes through `modeling-discipline` with its own
pre-registered bar, not through a good weekend.
